\chapter{Power Delivery and IR-Drop Analysis}
\label{ch:power}

\glance{PdnGen (\pkg{src/pdn}) synthesizes the power/ground grid (rings,
stripes, follow-pin rails, vias). PDNSim (\pkg{src/psm}) then solves the
resistive mesh for static IR drop and optional electromigration checks.
Power numbers come from OpenSTA \cmd{report\_power}.}

\section{Power grid generation: \pkg{src/pdn}}

\id{pdn::PdnGen} builds the PDN declaratively, then materializes it:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=ortcl]
add_global_connection -net VDD -pin_pattern {^VDD$} -power
add_global_connection -net VSS -pin_pattern {^VSS$} -ground
global_connect

define_pdn_grid -name main -pins {metal7}
add_pdn_ring   -grid main -layer {metal6 metal7} -widths 5.0 -spacings 3.0
add_pdn_stripe -grid main -layer metal1 -followpins
add_pdn_stripe -grid main -layer metal4 -width 0.48 -pitch 56.0
add_pdn_connect -grid main -layers {metal1 metal4}
add_pdn_connect -grid main -layers {metal4 metal7}
pdngen
\end{lstlisting}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Command} & \textbf{Role} \\
\midrule
\cmd{define\_pdn\_grid} & name a grid (core or \cmd{-macro}) \\
\cmd{add\_pdn\_ring} & rings around the core/macro \\
\cmd{add\_pdn\_stripe} & straps; \cmd{-followpins} = stdcell rails \\
\cmd{add\_pdn\_connect} & via stacks between layers \\
\cmd{pdngen} & emit special wires into odb \\
\cmd{add\_global\_connection}/\cmd{global\_connect} & tie instance PG pins to nets \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

Macro-specific grids (\cmd{define\_pdn\_grid -macro}) and power-switch
insertion are also supported for multi-voltage designs.

\section{IR-drop analysis: \pkg{src/psm}}

\id{psm::PDNSim} models the PG network as a resistive mesh, injects
per-instance current (from power estimates), and solves $G\,v = i$:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=ortcl]
analyze_power_grid -net VDD
check_power_grid -net VDD
write_pg_spice -net VDD pg.sp
\end{lstlisting}

Reports worst IR drop, current density, and floating stripes; \cmd{-enable\_em}
adds electromigration checks. \cmd{set\_pdnsim\_net\_voltage} sets the source
voltage.
