\chapter{Timing Optimization: The Resizer}
\label{ch:rsz}

\glance{The resizer (\pkg{src/rsz}, \id{rsz::Resizer}) is OpenROAD's timing-
closure engine. It sizes gates, inserts/removes buffers, clones loads, and
swaps pins --- always driven by OpenSTA slack and by wire RC from
estimation or extraction. It is invoked after placement, after CTS, and after
global route.}

\section{Where it sits}

\begin{lstlisting}[style=ortcl]
# typical call sites in a flow
estimate_parasitics -placement
repair_design
repair_timing -setup

clock_tree_synthesis
repair_timing -setup -hold

global_route
estimate_parasitics -global_routing
repair_timing -setup -hold
\end{lstlisting}

\id{OpenRoad::init()} wires the resizer to the DB, STA, Steiner builder,
global router, detailed placer, and parasitic estimator --- it is the biggest
consumer of the STA API.

\begin{lstlisting}[style=ortcl]
set_wire_rc -signal -layer metal3
estimate_parasitics -placement
remove_buffers
repair_design
repair_timing -setup -hold -repair_tns
\end{lstlisting}

\cmd{repair\_timing} options of note: \cmd{-setup}, \cmd{-hold},
\cmd{-skip\_pin\_swap}, \cmd{-repair\_tns}, and percent-end-point targets.
Hold fixing inserts delay buffers; setup fixing upsizes / inserts along the
Steiner topology of critical nets.

\section{Algorithms (conceptual)}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Gate sizing:} swap a cell for a stronger/weaker Liberty
        equivalent (respecting \cmd{dont\_use}).
  \item \textbf{Buffer insertion:} break long Steiner segments to meet slew/cap
        and improve delay.
  \item \textbf{Load splitting / cloning:} duplicate drivers for high fanout.
  \item \textbf{Pin swapping:} permute logically equivalent pins for slack.
\end{itemize}

All decisions are driven by STA arrival/required times under the currently
annotated parasitics (Ch.~\ref{ch:timing}).

\glance{\cmd{repair\_design} fixes electrical design-rule violations;
\cmd{repair\_timing} closes setup/hold. Call estimation (or extraction) before
either, or the resizer is optimizing the wrong RC.}
