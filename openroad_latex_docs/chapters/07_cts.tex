\chapter{Clock Tree Synthesis and Clock Gating}
\label{ch:cts}

\glance{TritonCTS (\pkg{src/cts}) builds a buffered, skew-balanced clock tree
on the fly (no offline characterization library required). Clock gating
(\pkg{src/cgt}) inserts integrated clock gates by proving enable conditions
with ABC, reducing dynamic power.}

\section{Clock tree synthesis: \pkg{src/cts} (TritonCTS)}

\id{cts::TritonCTS} is driven by \cmd{clock\_tree\_synthesis}. High-level flow:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Detect clock nets (or take \cmd{-clk\_nets}).
  \item Optionally cluster sinks (\cmd{-sink\_clustering\_enable}, size /
        diameter options) and macros separately.
  \item On-the-fly characterize candidate buffers for slew/cap (tunable via
        \cmd{configure\_cts\_characterization}).
  \item Build a buffered topology (branching / H-tree style), insert buffers
        from \cmd{-buf\_list} to meet transition and balance levels
        (\cmd{-balance\_levels}).
  \item Write the new buffers and nets into odb; optionally call
        \cmd{-repair\_clock\_nets} (resizer).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{lstlisting}[style=ortcl]
set_wire_rc -clock -layer metal5
configure_cts_characterization -max_slew 0.2 -max_cap 0.5
clock_tree_synthesis -buf_list {BUF_X1 BUF_X2 BUF_X4} \
                     -sink_clustering_enable \
                     -balance_levels \
                     -obstruction_aware
report_cts
\end{lstlisting}

Key options: \cmd{-root\_buf}, \cmd{-wire\_unit},
\cmd{-distance\_between\_buffers}, \cmd{-apply\_ndr}, \cmd{-insertion\_delay},
\cmd{-library}. CTS uses Steiner trees from \pkg{src/stt} and hands off to
\pkg{src/rsz} for post-CTS timing repair.

\section{Clock gating: \pkg{src/cgt}}

\id{cgt::ClockGating} automatically finds safe enable conditions and inserts
clock-gate cells. Per register it BFS-gathers nearby nets, exports a cone to
ABC (simulation then SAT) to prove an OR/AND enable is correct, minimizes the
net set, and --- if enough registers share a condition --- inserts a clock
gate.

\begin{lstlisting}[style=ortcl]
clock_gating -min_instances 10 -max_cover 100
\end{lstlisting}

ABC is used only as a proof engine here; its algorithms are covered in the
separate ABC reference.
