Methodological & Empirical Observations

1. Two-Sided Distribution & Regressions: Unlike synthetic sweeps clipped at zero, real EDA candidate sweeps produce degraded paths. 25% of candidate configurations worsen timing (P1: -1.79% WNS, -5.71% TNS), while top optimizations yield +0.74% (P95) and +1.26% (P99).

2. WNS vs. TNS Decoupling: Total Negative Slack (TNS) aggregates all violating endpoints across the clock domain, exhibiting ~3.3x greater variance (std dev 1.97% vs 0.60% WNS). Fixing a critical path often reveals secondary paths or spreads violations.

3. Timing vs. Tool Runtime Pareto Front: More aggressive gate sizing and buffer insertion algorithms introduce physical optimization effort, resulting in a positive correlation with runtime (P99 delta: +0.69s). No unphysical "free lunch" is observed.

4. Experimental Protocol & Baseline: Benchmarks evaluated on OpenROAD git commit 63fe72c across Nangate45 (gcd, aes) and ASAP7 (ibex). Noise floor across 3 seeds is ~0.05%.
