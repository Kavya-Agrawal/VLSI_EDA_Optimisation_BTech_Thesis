What the optimizer can change

Gate sizing

X1 → X2/X4 to drive load faster.

Buffering

Break a long or heavy net into stages.

Load splitting

Isolate non-critical sinks from a critical path.

Gate cloning

Duplicate a driver and divide fanout.

Pin / VT swap

Use a faster equivalent pin or cell variant.

Hold buffer

Add controlled delay to an overly fast path.

How success is measured

WNSWorst failing endpoint. Closer to zero is better.
TNSSum of all negative setup slack. Closer to zero is better.
HoldFast paths must still preserve old data at capture.
DRVNo new slew, capacitance, or fanout violations.
logical equivalencelegal placementdeterministicarea bounded

The research opportunity: improve steps 2–4—the target selection, move generation, and ranking heuristics—while the transactional step 6 prevents unsafe changes from entering the final design.
